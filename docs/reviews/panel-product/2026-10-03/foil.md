## Is this the real problem? (55-min / 5 Whys)
The panel calls it drift in emphasis and polish. That undersells it.

- **Why** is the differentiator under-invested? The roadmap defaults to theme breadth.
- **Why** does that matter? The one real user of the headline feature still can't do the job with ferrocv alone. The sibling `resume` repo keeps a second file, `resume-curated.json`, generated by `scripts/gen_curated.py` and committed next to the master. Its Makefile: *"Until ferrocv supports collapse-on-projection (cacack/ferrocv#196)."* The script docstring also cites #195.
- **Why** is that the root? §7 says the cut is "never a separately maintained file." The author's own resume is a separately maintained file. #196 is the bottleneck, and it is the lowest-priority open feature.

**Projection isn't finished for its first customer, and the roadmap is aimed somewhere else.** Fix that and most other gaps (positioning, audience-tag split, silent typos) become a short hardening sprint around it.

## The probes

**Voice of the Customer** — The only customer words are in the author's own Makefile: "until ferrocv supports #196". Sharper: make "the `resume` repo deletes `gen_curated.py` and `resume-curated.json`" the v0.10.0 exit criterion — it also answers "no Success Criteria" with one concrete check.

**Theory of Constraints / Complexity-vs-Value** — The constraint on adoption isn't theme count (`classic`, three adapters, `themes new` already ship). #101 is high-effort/low-priority → cut. #196 is medium-effort and carries the §7 thesis. Sharper: move #195/#196 into v0.10.0; move adapters to an unscheduled theme-breadth backlog, #97 (effort:low) optional quick win.

**Three benefits (speed, cost, risk)** — Five adapters don't ladder to any of the three; projection does (one edit regenerates every cut; master and cut can't drift; wrong bullets don't reach an employer).

**Risk: headline feature fails silently** — `tailor --audience securty` exits 0, no warning. On a document sent to an employer that is the worst kind of failure. Sharper: error (or at least stderr warning) when the audience matches no tag. Low effort; do it before anything cosmetic.

**§7 integrity (two audience vocabularies)** — `classic` reads `meta.x-audience` (`assets/themes/classic/resume.typ:132`, also `src/theme.rs:427`); `tailor` filters `x-ferrocv.audience` and never sets that label. Sharper: amend §7 so projection stamps an opaque display label into `meta`; themes may print it, never filter on it.

**Second-order thinking on #196** — "Collapse to one-liners" sits near the rewriting non-goal; next someone asks for "collapse to a summary sentence." Sharper: specify collapse as **field omission only** (keep name, position, dates; drop summary and highlights). No new text, ever.
